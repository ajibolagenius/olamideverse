Buttons

Real classes: ov-btn (shared shape) + one of ov-btn-danfo / ov-btn-ink / ov-btn-ghost / ov-btn-danger, defined once in globals.css and reused verbatim everywhere — nav CTAs, modal close, admin actions, admin destructive actions. Hover lifts the whole button and grows the hard shadow; press stamps it flat; disabled just fades to 50% opacity, no shadow change. One danfo-primary per viewport, everything else goes ink, ghost, or (destructive-only) danger.
