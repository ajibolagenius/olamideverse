Seventeen months after putting his own name on the spine, Olamide came back
with the opposite of a victory lap. *Time and Space* is ten tracks, announced
on 20 October 2026 with four weeks' notice and one single — "Burukutu," shot
by TG Omori — and released on 16 November 2026.

Where *Olamidé* gathered thirteen guests from Dr. Dre to Popcaan, this record
opens the door once: Fola, on "Jaiye." Everything else is him alone, from the
opener "Elohim" to the closer "Jogor."

Even the sleeve is his own hand. The cover is a portrait he painted himself,
hung in a gilt frame on a red wall.

This page is written on release day. The archive has the tracklist and the
embeds; it does not yet have the numbers, the full production credits, or the
reviews, and it doesn't guess. Those land here as they are verified.
